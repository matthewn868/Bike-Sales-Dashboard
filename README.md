# Bike Sales Dashboard — Excel Data Analysis

## Overview
An end-to-end data analysis project built in Microsoft Excel, exploring the 
customer demographics and behavioral patterns behind bike purchase decisions 
across 1,000 customers in North America, Europe, and the Pacific region.

## What I Built
- Cleaned and standardized raw customer data across multiple fields including 
  income, age, commute distance, occupation, and region
- Created an age brackets column to segment customers into Adolescent, 
  Middle Age, and Old categories for cleaner analysis
- Built pivot tables to surface key trends across income, commute distance, 
  and age groups
- Designed an interactive sales dashboard with slicers for dynamic filtering

## Key Findings
- Middle Age customers (31-60) drove the majority of bike purchases, 
  accounting for 383 out of 481 total purchases — over 79% of all buyers
- Customers who purchased bikes had a higher average income ($57,962) 
  compared to non-purchasers ($54,874) — suggesting income is a positive 
  indicator of purchase likelihood
- Male buyers had a notably higher average income ($60,123) than female 
  buyers ($55,774) among purchasers
- Short commute distance (0-1 miles) had the highest bike purchase volume 
  with 200 purchases — suggesting bikes are used for short distance commuting
- Purchase rates dropped significantly for commutes over 5 miles, with the 
  10+ miles group showing the lowest conversion (33 purchases vs 78 non-purchases)
- Adolescent customers had the lowest purchase rate — only 39 out of 110 
  (35%) made a purchase
- Old customers also underperformed with only 59 out of 189 (31%) purchasing

## Tools Used

1,000 customer records across Europe, North America, and Pacific regions
13 demographic and behavioral variables including income, age, education,
